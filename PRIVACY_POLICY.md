# Privacy Policy for YouTube Video Filter (Enhanced)

**Last Updated: April 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the "YouTube Video Filter (Enhanced)" browser extension (the "Extension") handles user data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
The Extension is designed to protect your privacy. 
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or browsing history).
- **Extension Settings:** The Extension stores your filter preferences, blocked channels, and custom rules. This data is used solely to provide the filtering functionality on YouTube.

## 2. Data Storage
- **Local Storage:** Your settings are stored locally within your browser using the `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **Synchronization:** If you are signed into your browser and have synchronization enabled, your settings are synced across your devices using the `chrome.storage.sync` API. This data is handled by Google's secure synchronization infrastructure.
- **No External Servers:** We do not maintain any external servers. Your data never leaves the Google ecosystem.

## 3. Third-Party Access
- **No Data Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools within the Extension.

## 4. User Control and Data Deletion
- You can view and modify your settings at any time through the Extension's popup menu.
- All data stored by the Extension is automatically deleted when you uninstall the Extension from your browser.
- You can manually clear all settings using the "Delete All Rules" or "Unblock All" buttons in the Extension's settings.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the feedback form provided within the Extension.
